<h3 class="major">Poster Presentation</h3>
The poster presentation felt like chaos in my head everyone walking around judging your poster before walking up asking you to run through the poster, especially as this was placed during dinner where they had lots and lots of food placed out and we as presenters had to be careful to not mistime our getting dinner. I did it turns out. but luckily for me I chatted to the people around me who let the judges know and me know so i made it back and made my presentation to the judges of the poster prizes. It was a pleasure to meet people like Stephen De Bank, learning about the vast array of research even in the field of photonics.

<h3 class="major"> Prize Giving</h3>
During the prize giving I was not expecting to win the prize so I was joking around with many people from the University of Bristol. So after a few wines it came as a large surprise to learn that my poster won the ISAT prize at Photon 24. This made me proud of the work I have performed and that my research efforts are appreciated in the wider world, and my communication skills being strong, giving me confidence to continue to perform interesting research.
