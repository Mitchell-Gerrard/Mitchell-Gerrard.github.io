Photon 24

A lesson in networking and scientific communication

My First Impressions

Currently working on the south coast of England gives me great appreciation for the seaside nature of Swansea. This is not the first time i have been to Swansea spending a year there during my undergraduate studies, I knew where to head first was the beach, due to the stony nature of my local beach the sand in Swansea is beautiful in the bay, giving space for students to hang out especially with the university so close to the sea.

<img src=”/confrences/Photon24/20180924_195842.jpg” alt=”Fire on Swansea Beach”,style=”max-width:100%;height:auto;”> So remembering the days I spent there I went to the beach to breath in the sea air. <img src=”/confrences/Photon24/20240904_113427.jpg” alt=”Swansea Beach”,style=”max-width:100%;height:auto;”> The next morning learning how much fun having a paid breakfast at a hotel is. Yummy! Having eggs Benedict, what a fantastic breakfast. <img src=”/Current_work/20240904_071900.jpg” alt=”Image of Eggs Benedict”,style=”max-width:100%;height:auto;”> Then making my way to the conference center which dint exist during my time as an undergraduate in the town, bringing my poster with me to put up ready for the presentations later in the week. Walking in there is the massive hall where the poster presentation would take place this is incredible i have never seen a room so big so empty just to show off lots of work by dedicated researchers. After pining up my poster, I went to my first talks, all of which were interesting learning about timing using lasers, how they reduce error at npl and the 50 year celebration my supervisors talk was part of. <img src=”/confrences/Photon24/20240904_133756.jpg” alt=”Image of Swansea conference hall”,style=”max-width:100%;height:auto;”>

Poster Presentation

The poster presentation felt like chaos in my head everyone walking around judging your poster before walking up asking you to run through the poster, especially as this was placed during dinner where they had lots and lots of food placed out and we as presenters had to be careful to not mistime our getting dinner. I did it turns out. but luckily for me I chatted to the people around me who let the judges know and me know so i made it back and made my presentation to the judges of the poster prizes. It was a pleasure to meet people like Stephen De Bank, learning about the vast array of research even in the field of photonics. <img src=”/confrences/Photon24/20240904_200232.jpg” alt=”Poster at the poster presentation”,style=”max-width:100%;height:auto;”>

Prize Giving

During the prize giving I was not expecting to win the prize so I was joking around with many people from the University of Bristol. So after a few wines it came as a large surprise to learn that my poster won the ISAT prize at Photon 24. This made me proud of the work I have performed and that my research efforts are appreciated in the wider world, and my communication skills being strong, giving me confidence to continue to perform interesting research. <img src=”/confrences/Photon24/20240904_200232.jpg” alt=”Photo of wine at the prize ceremony”,style=”max-width:100%;height:auto;”>

What I learned

Going to my first conference taught me a lot, from how to communicate with other researchers, how small and large the research network truly is and the breath of knowledge humanity has been able to obtain. It is incredible and I believe the next conference will be just as much fun as the first.
